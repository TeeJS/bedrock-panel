# Bedrock Panel v0.9.5-beta.2 — macOS beta, second build (Apple Silicon)

Everything from beta.1 (knob, touchscreen, Panel mode on the 1920×480 display, Reserved Display,
meeting recording with on-Mac transcription, the Music page, AI Voice with macOS speech), plus the
meeting features that were still Windows-only. Still a **beta for testers**: self-signed, not
notarized, so macOS asks you to allow it once, and a few permissions are granted by hand. Verified on
macOS 26 on an M-series MacBook Pro with the DK-QUAKE.

**Download:** `bedrock-panel-arm64.dmg` (Apple Silicon, macOS 14.2 or newer). The zip is the same app
without the disk image. Windows builds are unchanged (v0.9.4 remains current).

## New since beta.1

- **Meeting info from your calendar, on a Mac.** Settings → Meeting → Advanced → *Pull meeting
  information from my calendar* → source **macOS Calendar (this Mac)**. It reads today's meeting
  from macOS Calendar, so any account under System Settings → Internet Accounts works (Exchange /
  Microsoft 365, Google, iCloud). The first **Check Connection** shows the Calendars prompt: choose
  **Allow Full Access**, pick your account, and the calendar name fills itself in. Outlook for Mac
  keeps its calendar to itself, so if your work account cannot be added to macOS Calendar, pick the
  **Microsoft 365 (Graph)** source instead: install the Microsoft 365 app from Settings → Drop-In
  Apps → Browse, Connect on its page, then Check Connection here.
- **Zoom and Teams buttons on the Mac.** The Meeting page's Mute, Video, Accept, Decline, Hang Up,
  Share, and Full Screen now use the Mac shortcuts for Teams (Cmd-based), and for Zoom they press
  Zoom's own controls through Accessibility with Zoom left in the background: Mute Audio / Stop
  Video from the Meeting menu, and the meeting window's Leave / End button. Zoom not running, or no
  meeting, is reported on the panel. Windows behaviour is unchanged.
- **Every meeting button press is logged** in `main.log` with what it did, so a "did nothing"
  report can point at the line.

## First launch — five minutes, once

The full walkthrough, with every System Settings pane named, is **docs/macos.md**. In short:

1. Drag Bedrock Panel to Applications and launch it. macOS says it could not verify the app: click
   **Done**, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll to *Security* → **Open Anyway**.
2. Click **Always Allow** in the keychain dialog ("bedrock-panel Safe Storage") and type your login
   password. **This dialog returns once per new build** until the app carries an Apple Developer
   ID; the privacy grants below do not.
3. **Touchscreen:** the app opens System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Input Monitoring** and
   says so on the panel. Click **+**, pick Bedrock Panel from Applications, turn it on.
4. **Accessibility** (Reserved Display, keystroke tiles, macros, the Zoom and Teams buttons): the
   same **+** in the Accessibility pane.
5. **Microphone**, **Screen & System Audio Recording → System Audio Recording Only** (meeting
   recordings), and **Calendars** (meeting info), when macOS asks.

## Known limits in this beta

- Not notarized: the *Open Anyway* step on first launch after each download, and the keychain
  dialog once per build.
- Outlook for Mac shares no calendar with other apps: add the account to macOS Calendar or use the
  Microsoft 365 source (see above).
- The Zoom Phone accept/decline keys and all Teams controls are keystrokes; the meeting has to be in
  a normal window for them (Teams is brought to the front first).
- **Live speech recognition needs Dictation on** (System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation); file
  transcription does not.
- Spotify **Web** in a browser is not visible to the Music page; the Spotify desktop app is.
- A DisplayLink adapter shows macOS's purple screen-recording dot on the panel; it is not the app's.
- Windows-only for now: the touchscreen wizard, album-art thumbnails from the media session, and the
  community apps that need Windows executables.
