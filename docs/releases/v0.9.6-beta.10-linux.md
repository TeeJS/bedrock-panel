# Bedrock Panel v0.9.6-beta.10 — Linux beta (x64)

Bedrock Panel runs on Linux with the DK-QUAKE: Software mode, Panel mode on the 1920×480 display,
the knob and touchscreen, the tile grids, the editor, dashboards, and every platform-neutral app.
This is a **beta for testers**. Everything below is verified on Ubuntu 26.04 with KDE Plasma 6.6 on
Wayland, PipeWire, and a real DK-QUAKE.

## What changed since beta.9

**The Music page works, the volume rail reads the real volume, and a call can start a recording on
its own.** Three features that reported themselves unavailable on Linux now work, and none of them
needed anything installed — a Linux desktop already publishes all three.

- **Now playing**, with album art, and buttons that control the player being shown. It reads MPRIS
  over D-Bus, the same thing your desktop's own media applet reads, so Firefox, Chromium, VLC,
  Spotify and Elisa all appear. With two players open it follows the one actually playing.
- **The meeting console's output rail** shows the real system volume and follows the output you have
  selected.
- **Auto-record and the busy light** notice when an application takes the microphone. Set the app
  names under Settings → Meetings; on Linux these are program names like `zoom` or `chromium` rather
  than Windows `.exe` names.

**The three things Wayland genuinely forbids are now greyed out** with the reason beside them,
instead of accepting a setting and doing nothing: Reserved Display, follow the focused app, and
reading a calendar on this computer. The last one preselects Microsoft 365, which works everywhere.

## What changed earlier in this beta run

Five bugs from testing, all mine.

- **90 voices in 36 languages, up from 82 in 30.** My licence reader matched the words "CC BY" and
  missed every card that gives the licence as a bare URL instead, so shippable voices were quietly
  dropped across a dozen languages. British English had one voice; it has four.

- **Preview plays the voice you picked.** In beta.7 most previews were silent and the rest all played
  the same German voice. Asking "is this voice installed" answered yes for *every* voice, so Preview
  took the already-installed path for voices that were not installed, rewrote your chosen voice to
  one that did not exist, and the engine loaded the first voice in the folder instead — which is
  German. The published samples were never the problem; all 82 of them fetch real audio.

- **Preview works before you download at all.** It used to need the voice installed, which is useless for
  the one job it has: choosing between 82 voices by installing them 60 MB at a time is not choosing.
  Every Piper voice publishes a recording beside its model, so Preview plays that when the voice is
  not installed and speaks locally when it is.
- **Picking a voice works.** In beta.5 the list showed but the selection snapped back to the old
  voice, worst after changing language, where nothing in the new list could be chosen at all. The
  picker was asking the main process which voice was selected, and a pick is not saved until you
  press Save, so it kept getting the previous answer and writing it back over you.
- **Listening is a ladder of models, not one.** beta.5 offered a single multilingual model — the
  smallest one — which is why Live Translate was still rough after switching to it. There are now
  three, and the most accurate one is word-perfect on material the smallest one mangles.

## Choosing a listening model

**Settings → TTS/STT → Listening.** Measured on a 2019 laptop with no GPU, against a 13-second
German passage:

| model | download | speed | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| English only | 28 MB | fastest | English dictation and English meetings |
| Many languages — fastest | 111 MB | ~7× real time | loses a word, mangles compound words |
| Many languages — a step up | 198 MB | ~4× real time | same errors, slightly better |
| Many languages — most accurate | 610 MB | ~0.7× real time | word-perfect |

**If captions or transcripts read as fluent but wrong, that is the model being too small.** Move up
the ladder. The most accurate one is comfortable for meetings and dictation; for live captions on a
modest CPU it runs a beat behind.

Anything but English needs a *many languages* model. An English-only model does not fail on foreign
speech — it invents English out of the sounds, and whatever translates that downstream faithfully
translates the nonsense.

## What to try

- **Play something** in any player and watch the Music page: title, art, and the buttons.
- **Change the system volume** and watch the meeting console's output rail.
- **Start a call** in an app named in Settings → Meetings and see whether recording starts.
- **Preview voices you have not downloaded**, in a language you actually speak. Walk down a whole
  language's list; each one should sound different.
- **Change the voice language and pick a voice**, which was broken in beta.5.
- **Live Translate with the most accurate listening model.** This is the case beta.5 did not fix.
- **A meeting in a language other than English**, which uses the same model.
- Everything from beta.5: built-in speaking and listening,
  meeting transcription with named speakers, LucidType, the AI Voice apps, Monitor mode, global
  hotkeys and macros.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Whisper turbo and the large models are not offered.** Turbo matched the most accurate model
  exactly on the same audio while being larger and slower on a CPU; it pays off on a GPU, which this
  build does not assume anyone has. Point STT at your own server for those — yours still wins over
  the built-in engine.
- **The "distil" models are English-only** despite their names, so they are excluded: given German
  they translate it rather than transcribe it.
- **A person must be enrolled before a meeting transcript can name them.** Until then they are
  separated and numbered.
- **Someone who speaks for less than five seconds in a whole meeting** is not reported as a
  participant; their words fall back to the generic label.
- **A display scaled to anything other than 100% is untested** with Monitor mode.
- **Reserved Display**, **follow the focused app** and **Teams window control** are not available;
  Wayland does not allow moving other applications' windows.
- **Now playing and system volume** are still not wired up. The media *keys* work.
- **Outlook desktop meeting info** is Windows-only; use the Microsoft 365 source.
- **AppImage needs FUSE 2** — install `libfuse2t64` or use the `.deb`.
- x64 only. No arm64 build yet.
