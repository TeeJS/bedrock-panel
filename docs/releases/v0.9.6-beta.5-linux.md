# Bedrock Panel v0.9.6-beta.5 — Linux beta (x64)

Bedrock Panel runs on Linux with the DK-QUAKE: Software mode, Panel mode on the 1920×480 display,
the knob and touchscreen, the tile grids, the editor, dashboards, and every platform-neutral app.
This is a **beta for testers**. Everything below is verified on Ubuntu 26.04 with KDE Plasma 6.6 on
Wayland, PipeWire, and a real DK-QUAKE.

## What changed since beta.4

beta.4 could speak and listen, but only in English, and it showed.

- **82 voices in 30 languages**, chosen by language first. **Preview** speaks a line in a voice you
  have downloaded, and the hint links to samples of every voice for choosing beforehand. beta.4
  offered four English voices, which is not a voice list.
- **Listening can be multilingual.** A second recognition model is on offer. **Live Translate needs
  it**, and so does any meeting or dictation that is not in English.
- **Live Translate works.** Two bugs, both mine. The English-only model does not fail on foreign
  speech, it invents English out of the sounds — "Guten Morgen, wir beginnen die Besprechung mit dem
  Quartalsbericht" came back as *"Good morning, we begin the vascation with incorrect answer"*, and
  the translator downstream faithfully translated the nonsense. And the **source language** setting
  never reached the recognizer at all, so the one control that should have fixed it could not.

**Download:** `bedrock-panel_amd64.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu, or `bedrock-panel-x86_64.AppImage`
for anything else. Windows and macOS builds are unchanged in this beta (v0.9.5 remains current).

## Choosing a voice and a listening model

**Settings → TTS/STT.**

- **Voice** — pick a language, then a voice. Every one is public domain, CC0, CC BY or MIT; the
  licence is shown beside each. Most are about 60 MB. **Preview** plays a line once it is downloaded.
- **Listening** — two choices:
  - *English only* — 28 MB, the fastest, fine for English dictation and English meetings.
  - *Many languages* — about 110 MB, a little slower, and **required for Live Translate** or for
    listening to anything but English.

Both halves remain optional and independent, and a server of your own on 127.0.0.1:10200 or :10300
still wins over the built-in one.

## What to try

- **Pick a voice in your own language** and press Preview.
- **Live Translate** with the *Many languages* model installed, with and without the source language
  set. It should be right either way; Whisper detects the language well on its own.
- **A meeting in a language other than English**, once the multilingual model is installed.
- Everything from beta.4: built-in speaking and listening, meeting transcription with named speakers,
  LucidType dictation, the AI Voice apps, Monitor mode, global hotkeys and macros.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Preview needs the voice downloaded first.** A voice cannot be heard before it exists locally;
  the samples link in the hint is for comparing before you commit to a download.
- **The multilingual model is a *tiny* Whisper.** It is accurate enough for captions and dictation
  and will misplace an occasional compound word. Bigger models exist; if the accuracy matters more
  than the download, point STT at your own server.
- **Voices are one per speaker at the balanced quality.** The "high" versions are roughly twice the
  download for a difference that a panel speaker will not reproduce.
- **A person must be enrolled before a meeting transcript can name them.** Until then they are
  separated and numbered.
- **Someone who speaks for less than five seconds in a whole meeting** is not reported as a
  participant; their words fall back to the generic label rather than inventing a person.
- **A display scaled to anything other than 100% is untested** with Monitor mode.
- **Reserved Display**, **follow the focused app** and **Teams window control** are not available;
  Wayland does not allow moving other applications' windows.
- **Now playing and system volume** are still not wired up, so the Music page is blank and the
  meeting console's output rail shows a dash. The media *keys* work.
- **Outlook desktop meeting info** is Windows-only; use the Microsoft 365 source.
- **AppImage needs FUSE 2** — install `libfuse2t64` or use the `.deb`.
- x64 only. No arm64 build yet.
