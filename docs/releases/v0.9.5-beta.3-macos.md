# Bedrock Panel v0.9.5-beta.3 — macOS beta, third build (Apple Silicon)

Everything from beta.2, plus fixes from a day of testing the voice pages and the Music page on a
Mac. Still a **beta for testers**: self-signed, not notarized, so macOS asks you to allow it once,
and a few permissions are granted by hand. Verified on macOS 26 on an M-series MacBook Pro.

**Download:** `bedrock-panel-arm64.dmg` (Apple Silicon, macOS 14.2 or newer). The zip is the same app
without the disk image. Windows builds are unchanged (v0.9.4 remains current).

## New since beta.2

- **Live speech recognition on macOS 26 uses Apple's SpeechAnalyzer** — the engine that already
  makes the meeting transcripts. Live Translate, AI Voice, and LucidType stop losing short
  utterances (the old recognizer answered most 1–3 s phrases with "no speech"), there is no
  Dictation setting to turn on and no speech prompt, and each phrase comes back in well under half
  a second. Each language's model downloads once, on first use (about a minute; the page waits).
  Covered: German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese,
  Cantonese. Other languages fall back to the older recognizer, which needs Dictation on.
- **Live Translate knows what language is spoken.** The Mac engine cannot detect it, so a new
  **Spoken language** row on the page's Settings card (Auto plus 28 languages) tells it — and the
  interpreter. A bare code such as `de` means the home region (Germany, Spain, France, Brazil for
  `pt`); `de-AT`-style codes are used as given. With a Whisper or Soniox server, Auto still works.
- **Music page shows what is already playing.** A player that was running before Bedrock Panel
  started used to stay invisible until the next track change. The monitor now asks Spotify or
  Music directly at start, when the player launches, and every 10 s. The first time, macOS asks
  "Bedrock Panel wants to control Spotify" (or Music): allow it.

## First launch — five minutes, once

The full walkthrough, with every System Settings pane named, is **docs/macos.md**. In short:

1. Drag Bedrock Panel to Applications and launch it. macOS says it could not verify the app: click
   **Done**, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll to *Security* → **Open Anyway**.
2. Click **Always Allow** in the keychain dialog ("bedrock-panel Safe Storage") and type your login
   password. This dialog returns once per new build until the app carries an Apple Developer ID.
3. **Touchscreen:** the app opens System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Input Monitoring** and
   says so on the panel. Click **+**, pick Bedrock Panel from Applications, turn it on.
4. **Accessibility** (Reserved Display, keystroke tiles, macros, the Zoom and Teams buttons): the
   same **+** in the Accessibility pane.
5. **Microphone**, **Screen & System Audio Recording → System Audio Recording Only** (meeting
   recordings), **Calendars** (meeting info), and **Automation** (Music page), when macOS asks.

## Known limits in this beta

- Not notarized: the *Open Anyway* step on first launch after each download, and the keychain
  dialog once per build.
- Outlook for Mac shares no calendar with other apps: add the account to macOS Calendar or use the
  Microsoft 365 source.
- Spotify **Web**, YouTube Music, and other browser players are not visible to the Music page; the
  Spotify desktop app and Music are.
- The Zoom Phone accept/decline keys and all Teams controls are keystrokes; Teams is brought to the
  front first.
- A DisplayLink adapter shows macOS's purple screen-recording dot on the panel; it is not the app's.
- Windows-only for now: the touchscreen wizard, album-art thumbnails from the media session, and the
  community apps that need Windows executables.
