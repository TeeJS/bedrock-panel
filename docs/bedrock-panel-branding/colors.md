## Roles

- Light primary CTA: #285A87 fill, white label. Links and logo mark: #285A87. Body and wordmark: #202C3A. Secondary actions and supporting text: #54657B on the light surface.
- Dark primary CTA: #99C8F0 fill, #202C3A label. Links and mark: #99C8F0. Body and wordmark: #F5F7FA. Secondary labels: #C3CDD9.
- Keep inline links underlined. Use icons/labels as well as hue for states. Semantic green, amber and red are functional status colors, not competing brand accents.
- Pale blue #99C8F0 on #F5F7FA is 1.65:1; on white it is 1.77:1. Do not use it for light-mode text or meaningful icons/borders.

## Verified light-mode contrast

| Foreground | On #F5F7FA | On white |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #202C3A body / wordmark | 13.20:1 AAA | 14.16:1 AAA |
| #285A87 mark / links | 6.74:1 AA | 7.24:1 AAA |
| #54657B supporting text | 5.55:1 AA | 5.96:1 AA |

White text on #285A87: 7.24:1 (AAA). Normal text AA requires 4.5:1; AAA requires 7:1. The report contains recomputed values, including semantic text pairs. A palette contrast check is not a full application accessibility audit.

Reference: [WCAG 2.2 contrast requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum).

## Print
